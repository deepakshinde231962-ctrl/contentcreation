**Book = 90 saal purana "behavioural finance" ka syllabus. Jo niyam isme hain, unki *spirit* scientifically

## 3. Niyam-by-niyam: kya scientifically strong hai

| # | Book ka niyam (Clason) | Evidence status | Aaj ka translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pay yourself first (10%)** | **Strong** — automatic/default saving par sabse zyada field evidence. Madrian & Shea (2001): auto-enrolment se naye hires ki participation 37% → 86%. Thaler & Benartzi (2004, *Save More Tomorrow*): 28 mahine me average saving rate 3.5% → 11.6%; 78% ne join kiya, 80% 4 raises tak bane rahe. | Salary ke agle din auto-debit. |
| 2 | **Control thy expenditures** | **Supported** — mental accounting + "spending follows the visible balance". | Fixed & variable kharche alag karo, 50/30/20 jaisa envelope. |
| 3 | **Make thy gold multiply** | **Supported** — compounding + long horizon. | SIP/index-style, goal-based. (Video me koi scheme name nahi.) |
| 4 | **Guard thy treasures from loss** | **Very strong** — "invest in what you understand" = classic risk & fraud-avoidance advice. | SEBI-registered products, guaranteed-return scams se door. |
| 5 | **Make of thy dwelling a profitable investment** | **Mixture** — India me ghar *emotional + tax* fayda deta hai, par 20-30 saal ka EMI opportunity cost bhi hai. 2026 me "ghar lena zaroori" nahi, "ghar afford karna" zaroori hai. | Rent vs buy ka honest math. |
| 6 | **Insure a future income** | **Strong** — retirement ke liye automatic, escalating contribution. | NPS/EPF + term insurance + health cover. |
| 7 | **Increase thy ability to earn** | **Strongest ROI** — skill/human capital par kharch, especially early career. | Har mahine ek skill par fixed budget. |

**Conclusion:** Clason ka genius *percentage* nahi, **sequence** hai — save → phir kharch. Aaj ka behavioural
science isi sequence ko "commitment device" kehta hai.

## 4. Kahan FAIL hota hai (video ka "fine print" section)

### 4.1 Inflation (sabse bada loophole)
Clason ke Babylon me mehangai ka koi zikr nahi. Aaj agar 10% bachat 3-4% return wale savings account me
padi rahe aur inflation 5-6% ho, to **real return negative** ho sakta hai. `[CHECK]` — current India CPI
aur savings-account rates video ke publish date par verify karna. (Video me hum sirf *principle* bolte hain,
koi number nahi.)

### 4.2 Low income / cash-flow shock
"10% nikaal do" ek aisa household ke liye cruel advice hai jahan 10% nikaalne se kiraya ya dawai ka paisa
kat jaata hai. Evidence: auto-enrolment ka **downside** bhi documented hai — jo log auto-enrol hote hain,
wo default 3% par atak jaate hain (Madrian & Shea). Yani default achha hai, par **default ka level** bhi
matter karta hai.

### 4.3 Survivorship bias in the parables
Kahani me har kirdar jo niyam maanta hai, ameer ho jaata hai. Asli duniya me:
- Medical emergency ek saving household ko ek jhatke me zero kar sakti hai (isliye emergency fund pehle).
- Job loss, business failure, family dependency — inka koi zikr book me nahi.
- Ek honest review ne ise theek kaha: "Clason's Babylon has no such thing, and a reader in a genuinely hard
  season may find the confidence grating."

### 4.4 Dated mechanics (2026 India)
Book me nahi hai: **tax, inflation, credit score, health insurance, term insurance, mutual funds, index
funds, UPI/auto-debit, retirement accounts, gig income, student loans, medical inflation, digital fraud.**
Yani book *kya* karna hai batati hai, *kaise* nahi.

### 4.5 The "10%" is arbitrary — the *automatic* part is what works
Madrian–Shea ka data dikhata hai ki default rate par log atak jaate hain. Isliye "10%" ko jaadu mat banao;
**rate ko har raise ke saath badhao** (Save More Tomorrow ka core idea). Isi liye video me hum "10% + raise
ka aadha hissa" recommend karte hain.

### 4.6 India-specific traps jo book me nahi ho sakte the
- **Credit card revolving interest (~36-48% p.a.)** — book ke "usury" wale warning ka modern version.
- **F&O / intraday**: SEBI (Sep 2024) study — FY22–FY24 me **93% individual F&O traders ne paisa khoya**,
  aggregate loss **₹1.8 lakh crore+**. (Source: SEBI Press Release 22/2024.) Ye Clason ke "guard thy
  treasures from loss" ka sabse taaza proof hai.
- **Financial-literacy gap**: RBI/NCFE surveys India me financial literacy ko ~27-30% range me dikhate hain
  `[CHECK]` exact latest figure — video me cite nahi kiya.

## 5. Scientific upgrades (2026 version)

1. **Default > discipline.** Auto-debit salary ke agle din (ya direct-deposit split).
2. **Escalate automatically.** Har increment ka aadha hissa saving me (Save More Tomorrow).
3. **Emergency fund pehle, investment baad me.** 3 mahine ka kharch; ₹500 se shuru bhi valid hai.
4. **Inflation se ladne wali jagah** — par product recommendation nahi, sirf "risk aur horizon" ka framework.
5. **Sabse mehnga debt pehle.** Credit card revolving balance ko priority.
6. **Human capital.** Har mahine ek skill par kharch — Clason ka 7th cure, aur empirically highest ROI.
7. **Review, not willpower.** Mahine me ek baar 10-minute statement review (hiding se hi overspend badhta hai).
