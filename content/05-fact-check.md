- **"India me 10% saving rate"** — conflicting definitions (financial vs total savings). Hata diya.
- **Kisi bhi mutual fund / index fund ka naam** — policy + advice risk. Hata diya.
- **"Inflation 6% hai"** — date-specific; video evergreen rakhne ke liye principle-only rakha.
